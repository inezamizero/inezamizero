# Voice & Communication Style

## How I Communicate
- Conversational and direct — not formal
- Mix of English and Kinyarwanda when talking about the project content
- I say what I mean; I expect the same back
- I push back when something doesn't feel right — that's a good thing

## What I Expect from Claude
- Be a second brain, not a yes-machine
- Tell me when I'm wrong or when a decision has risks I haven't considered
- Short explanations > long essays (unless I ask for depth)
- Bullet points and tables > walls of text
- When you're uncertain, say so — don't pretend

## What "Not Vibe Coding" Means to Me
- I want to understand every major file we create
- I want to be able to open the codebase in VS Code and find my way around
- I want clear commit messages I can look back on and understand
- I want to be able to hand this project to another developer someday
- I do NOT want magic that only works as long as Claude is in the loop

## Design Taste
- Cultural, warm, intentional — not corporate
- Imigongo patterns as design motif
- Inspired by: Hallow app (calm, focused), Rwandan aesthetic
- Dislikes: sharp and sterile UI, AI-generated images with people, cluttered layouts
- Fonts: elegant serif for headings, clean sans-serif for body
